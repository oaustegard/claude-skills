# Fable 5.1

## As author

Fable 5.1 is the least staged model measured and the least detectable. In the
model-register-drift addendum (`oaustegard/experiments`,
`model-register-drift/FABLE-ADDENDUM.md`, 2026-09-01) its two samples scored
−2.06 on staging (both sample intervals below zero) and −2.52 on
AI-detectability; one sample was judged human in all twelve comparisons. Em
dashes were near zero. The Opus line's rise in staging with each version does
not carry to Fable.

Residual register in both samples:

- Bold-lead enumerated causes and bolded maxim takeaways (entries 29, 30, 12).
- An "X, not Y" closer (entries 2 and 12).
- The literal phrase "the one that stung" / "the part that stung" in both
  independent samples (entry 3). The linter catches "the part that"; it does not
  catch "the one that", because that phrase is usually literal. Read for it.

Lint was uneven across the two samples, 3.05 and 9.92 candidates per 1000 words,
with `header` and `triad` the top categories.

What this changes: a lighter pass. Most edits will be typography and headers.
Watch the bolded takeaway line at the end of a list; it is where Fable puts its
maxims.

Evidence is two samples from a reconstructed prompt, judged by API rather than
subagent. Weaker than the Opus and Sonnet evidence.

# The haiku-cc run, 2026-10-12: every failure, its signal, and what now handles it

Haiku 5.5 workers built hcc (a C11 compiler in pure Python) against ~42K tests, from
an Opus-built foundation. Hub plus three sibling containers from 11:27Z. By 18:30Z the
hub ledger held 1,051 workers, 464 merged, $24.88 of worker spend ($0.054 per merge),
and 42,189 of 42,358 tracked tests passed. Every item below cost time or money that
day. Kind: **P** prevented in code, **D** detected by a named signal, **W** warned in
the runbook with its signal and fix. Paths are relative to this skill.

## Model measurements (escalation runs 1-3)

Same prompt, budget and pinned base; each patch graded in isolation (a representative
flips, nothing in smoke + a passing sample regresses).

| run | clusters (after base check) | Haiku | Haiku high | Sonnet medium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1: 40 random failing clusters | 40 | 76/80 runs, $0.81, $0.011/solve | — | 80/80, $6.01, $0.075/solve |
| 2: clusters Haiku had failed | 4 (of 18 selected) | 8/8, $0.10, $0.012/solve | 8/8, $0.18, $0.023/solve | 8/8, $0.61, $0.077/solve |
| 3: the tail the swarm abandoned | 10 (of 33 selected) | 19/20, $0.22, $0.012/solve | 20/20, $0.35, $0.018/solve | 20/20, $1.74, $0.087/solve |

Over 54 clusters, cold Sonnet solved 2 that cold Haiku did not (run 1, c06 and c16).
Haiku feature-mode workers in the live swarm later fixed both, for $0.054 and $0.022:
the retry path (notes from failed attempts, feature budget, effort ladder) closed both
gaps. Run 3's ten clusters were the swarm's abandoned tail, and Haiku solved them cold
once the harness bugs that parked them were fixed: that tail was harness-made. Scope:
one compiler workload, small n. Sources: `oaustegard/experiments` 9b1d4d0,
`haiku-cc/RESULTS.md`; Muninn memories 5a6b348d, 8bcf09e0, fd5ae046 (supersedes ad0c7ea8).

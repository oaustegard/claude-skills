# write: a text

Markdown as it stands. `# Words {.title}` is the title, set large; `#` and `##` are headings; `{.center}`, `{.right}` or `{.justify}` at the end of a paragraph aligns it. A table works out its formulas. A fence named by any app's id (```` ```anim ````, `draw`, `diagram`, `sheet`, `trip`, `cal`, the lot) puts that document in the page as a figure, drawn by its app. `paper: a4` or `letter` in the head sets the paper (a reader in North America gets letter otherwise). Notes are listed at the end, in the editor, on paper, in the web page and in Word. an invoice is a text with its lines as a table.

Meeting notes:

```
# Meeting {.title}

*The date · where · who came*

## Agenda

1. *First item*
2. *Second item*

## Notes

*What was said, briefly.*

## Decisions

- *What was decided.*

## Actions

- [ ] *Who does what, by when*
```

Templates: A letter, Cover letter, Memo, Meeting notes, Minutes, Résumé, One-page CV, Invoice, Contract, Newsletter, Recipe, Questions and answers, Press release, News article, Interview, Book review, Music review, Thesis, Grant proposal, Syllabus, Formal complaint, Letter to a politician, Love letter, Wedding speech, Eulogy, Zine essay, Petition, Terms of service. Its own files: `.docx`, `.md`, `.rtf`, `.html`.
